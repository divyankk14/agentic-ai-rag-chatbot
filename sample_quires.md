**Answer:** Challenges include maintaining system reliability during partial failures, ensuring one agent's failure doesn't jeopardize overall system performance, orchestrating large-scale systems with hundreds/thousands of agents, accommodating agent additions/removals without disruption, resolving inter-agent conflicts without human intervention, choosing the right agent among multiple capable ones, and managing communication and resource allocation.
